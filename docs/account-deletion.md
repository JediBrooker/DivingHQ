# Delete your DivingHQ account

You can request account deletion without signing in or reinstalling the DivingHQ app. [Request account deletion by email](mailto:support@divinghq.app?subject=DivingHQ%20account%20deletion) at **support@divinghq.app**. DivingHQ (divinghq.app) operates this service.

Tell us your DivingHQ username and the email address on the account. We will check that the request is yours before taking action and answer within 30 days. **Do not email your password or two-factor codes.** Sending the email starts a support request; it does not automatically delete the account.

## Delete it yourself

In the website or app, sign in and open **Profile → Delete account**. Enter your password when asked and confirm deletion. You can [sign in on the website](/login) to do this even if the app has been uninstalled.

If you are the only administrator left in your federation, appoint another administrator first. If you have nobody to hand over to, email support using the request link above: we can help remove the administrator role so deletion can proceed.

## What is removed

Account deletion removes your login credentials, email, two-factor setup, date of birth, gender, nationality, language and dashboard settings. It revokes account roles and personal links, disables push delivery, removes your public profile, and signs out active sessions on every device. Pending role, club and organisation requests are removed or closed as described in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## What is retained

- **Named sporting records:** your name, club or country on historical competition entries, dive lists, scores, rankings and judging records remain in the sporting archive. They no longer link to an active personal profile. Deletion retains a record identifier and the name and organisation/club association needed by the record system; it does not erase every database row.
- **Native notification records:** delivery stops, but the installation identifier, device token, former account association and delivery diagnostics can remain without an automatic purge schedule. You can request their removal by email.
- **Audit and security records:** retained for the normal 30-day period for integrity, dispute handling and security.
- **Backups:** deleted data can remain in access-restricted backups until they expire, at most about five weeks later.
- **Exports you saved or shared:** copies outside DivingHQ remain in the destination you chose.

If a sporting result or other retained information needs to be removed, including for a child-protection concern or mistaken identity, include that in your email. We will work with the organisation that ran the event. You can also request access, correction or removal of particular personal data without closing your account.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for the full retention details, or [contact support](/support) for help.
