# Quick Start

This takes a club from nothing to its first scored meet on [divinghq.app](https://divinghq.app). Your own setup takes about ten minutes. The rest is waiting for your members to sign up.

You don't need your federation or state body to be on DivingHQ first. A club can start on its own, run its own meets, and hand over to its federation later if and when that body joins. If you *are* a federation or state body, skip to [Federations and state bodies](#federations-and-state-bodies) at the end.

## 1. Create your account and your club

![The sign-up page, with country, club and role pickers](/guide-screenshots/register.png)

Go to [Create an account](/register) and fill in your name, a username, your email address and a password. Then:

- **Country.** Pick your country. The page tells you whether anyone from it is already on DivingHQ.
- **State or region.** Countries with states, provinces or home nations (Australia, Canada and the UK so far) ask which one you're in.
- **Club.** If your club is already listed, pick it: its admin approves you. If it isn't, choose **+ Create a new club** and give it a name and an optional short code (3 to 6 letters, e.g. `SYD`). The short code is the chip shown next to your divers on the scoreboard.
- **I want to register as.** Diver is the default. Pick what you'll actually do; everyone starts as a spectator and gets more once approved.

In a country with no federation on DivingHQ yet, **creating a club makes you its club admin** straight away. In a country whose federation already runs DivingHQ, a new club waits for the federation to approve it (unless it lets clubs join automatically). It asks them once you've confirmed your email, and you can sign in and dive while they decide. When they approve it they usually make you its admin; otherwise the federation decides who admins each club. Your dashboard shows the club as waiting until then, and you'll get an email either way.

## 2. Confirm your email

We send a "confirm your email" message with a link. Click it, then sign in at [Sign in](/login). You can't sign in until the address is confirmed.

Nothing arrived after a few minutes? Check your spam folder. Then try signing in: the sign-in page offers **Send a new verification link**.

## 3. Bring in your members

Copy your club's **invite link** from **My club** (`/club`, in the sidebar) and send it to your members. It opens the sign-up form with your country and club already picked. Each person chooses the role they want: diver, coach, judge or referee.

Their requests land on My club too. Approve or reject each one there. A referee request is the exception: in a country with no federation on DivingHQ yet it goes to DivingHQ, because a referee can act at any club's meet in the country.

For a first scored meet you need at least:

- **5 judges** (the usual panel for an individual event; synchro events need 7, 9 or 11)
- **1 referee**, who signs the panel off before the event can start
- **a few divers**

While your country has no federation on DivingHQ, My club is also where you add **co-admins**: other members who can help you run the club. Two admins is a good idea, so the club isn't stuck if one of you is away.

Club admins also get a **Get started** checklist at the top of the dashboard: create your first meet, invite your members, set your club's short code (on **My club**, up to 8 letters, numbers or dashes, e.g. `SYD`), and read the club admin guide. Each step links to where it's done and ticks itself off; **Hide** puts the checklist away on that device. Under a federation, the federation sets club codes from its Clubs screen instead.

See [Roles & Permissions](/guide/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## 4. Create your meet and its events

![Meet Manager, listing the meets and their events with per-event action buttons](/guide-screenshots/meet-manager.png)

Open **Meet Manager** (`/manager`). The left rail lists your meets; the right pane shows the selected meet's events.

1. Click **+ New meet**, give it a name, venue and dates. As a club admin, the meet is hosted by your club. (If you admin more than one club or a region, you pick which.)
2. Open the meet's **Edit** dialog and set **Divers represent**: their club for club and inter-club meets, their state or province for a national championship, or their country for an international meet. That's the chip shown next to each diver on the scoreboard, results and PDFs, and what the medal table groups by.
3. Click **+ New event** and fill in:
   - **Event Name**, e.g. "Women 3 m Springboard"
   - **Event Type**: Individual, Synchro Pair or Team
   - **Gender** and **Board / Platform Height**
   - **Judge Panel Size**: 5 for most individual events; 7, 9 or 11 for synchro
   - **Rounds**: add one row per round with **+ Add Dive**
4. **Entries.** Divers submit their own dive lists from **Submit Dive Sheets** (`/competitor`) while the event is Upcoming and entries are open. You can also add divers yourself, or import a CSV roster from the event's **Edit** dialog.
5. **Judges.** From the event row, open **Assign Judges** and put the panel in order (Judge 1 first).

![The Assign Judges page, with the available judges on one side and the ordered panel slots on the other](/guide-screenshots/assign-judges.png)

For synchro events, the panel positions map to roles:

| Panel size | Exec A | Exec B | Sync |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 1 – 2 | 3 – 4 | 5 – 7 |
| 9 | 1 – 2 | 3 – 4 | 5 – 9 |
| 11 | 1 – 3 | 4 – 6 | 7 – 11 |

Running several events across a day? **Schedule** (on the meet) plans boards, warmups and breaks and flags clashes. See [Session Scheduler](/guide/session-scheduler).

## 5. Run it from the Control Room

On the day, open the event from your dashboard's **Open Control Room** card, or from its row in Meet Manager. Judges sign in on their phones and open **Judge Terminal**; the active diver appears on their screen automatically.

Before the start, the Control Room shows a **readiness checklist** with one button for the next thing to do:

1. **✓ Check In Divers.** Tick everyone who's present.
2. **🎲 Randomise Dive Order.**
3. **📋 Referee Sign Off.** The referee scans a QR code on your screen with their phone (or types a 6-digit code) to confirm the panel.
4. **▶ Start Event.** The event goes Live and the public scoreboard starts updating.

![Control Room before an event starts, showing the readiness checklist above its contextual action button](/guide-screenshots/control-room-premeet-checklist.png)

From then on you step through the dive order; scores arrive from the judges' phones and appear on the scoreboard within a second. [Running a Meet](/guide/running-a-meet) covers holds, redives, score corrections and late entries.

Anyone can follow along at **Scoreboard & Results** (`/scoreboard`), no account needed. Use the **Broadcast** view on a projector or TV at the pool.

![The live scoreboard: the current performer in the centre column, judge scores beneath, and live standings alongside](/guide-screenshots/scoreboard-live.png)

When the last dive is scored, click **Finalise Event**. The scoreboard switches to the results recap, the event joins the public archive, the programme, start list, score sheet and results PDFs are one click away, and any record the meet set shows up in the record books at [Records](/records).

## Federations and state bodies

If you represent a national federation or a state, provincial or home-nation body, register at [Register your org](/register-org) and say which country, and optionally which region, you represent.

- **Clubs from your country are already on DivingHQ.** Your registration opens a **claim** to take over the account they started. Once you confirm your email, the clubs (or, for a national claim, the state bodies already here) vote on it on **Claims**. If there aren't enough eligible voters, or anyone objects, DivingHQ decides. A state body in a country whose federation already runs DivingHQ is approved by that federation instead. Once it's approved you become its federation admin, or region admin for a state, and everything the clubs built stays in place: meets, results, records and members.
- **Nobody from your country is here yet.** DivingHQ reviews your registration first. Once it's approved, clubs from your country that sign up join your federation directly.

Either way, [Roles & Permissions](/guide/roles-and-permissions#claims) explains what changes once you're in, and [Admin Tasks](/guide/admin-tasks) covers running a federation day to day.
