# Junior Divers (under 13)

DivingHQ accounts are for people aged 13 and over, but plenty of divers start younger. A diver under 13 gets a **junior profile** instead: a parent (or their coach, or their club) adds it, the club confirms it, and from then on the diver can be entered in meets like anyone else. Nobody signs in to a junior profile. Their meet notifications come to you, the parent, and when they turn 13 they take the profile over with all their results.

This page is mostly for parents. Coaches, club admins and federation admins have their own sections near the end.

## At a glance

| Step | Who does it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Make your own account | You, the parent | [Create an account](/register) |
| 2. Add your child | You | **Junior divers** |
| 3. Confirm your child | Your child's club, or the federation | **My club**, or **User Manager** |
| 4. Enter meets | Your child's coach, or the meet's organisers | Their usual screens |
| 5. Hand over at 13 | You, or the club | **Junior divers** |

## Before you start: your own account

You need a DivingHQ account of your own first. [Create an account](/register), pick your country, and choose **Spectator only** under **I want to register as** (you don't need a role to look after a child). You don't need to be in the club yourself: leave **Club** on **No club / independent** if you don't dive. Confirm your email address, then sign in.

Have these ready for each child:

- their **full name**, exactly as it should appear on results
- their **date of birth**
- their **gender** (it decides which events they can be entered in)
- their **club**

## Adding your child

Open **Junior divers**. It's in the sidebar under **Account**; on a phone, tap **Menu** at the bottom, then **Junior divers**. Fill in **Add a junior diver**:

![The "Add a junior diver" form with a club picked, the child's name, date of birth and gender filled in, "Their parent or guardian" selected and the agreement ticked](/guide-screenshots/juniors-add-child.png)

1. **Club.** Pick your child's club. Only clubs from your own country are listed.
2. **Full name**, **Date of birth** and **Gender**.
3. **You're adding them as.** Choose **Their parent or guardian**.
4. **Tick the agreement.** You're confirming you're the child's parent or legal guardian, and that you agree to their name, date of birth and results being kept on DivingHQ. Who ticked it and when is recorded.
5. Press **Add junior diver**.

Got more than one child under 13? Add each of them the same way. A child who is 13 or over can't be added here: they sign up for an account of their own, ideally with you alongside.

## What happens next: the club confirms

Your child shows up at the top of the page, **Waiting to be confirmed**:

![A junior card for Mia Fischer, aged 9, at Bondi Diving Club, with the badge "Waiting to be confirmed" and a Withdraw button](/guide-screenshots/juniors-waiting.png)

Someone who looks after the club's divers now checks that your child really dives there and that you are who you say you are. Who that is depends on your country:

| Your country on DivingHQ | Who confirms | Where they do it |
|---|---|---|
| No federation on DivingHQ yet (the clubs run things) | Your child's club admins. If the club has nobody, the region's admins, then the DivingHQ team | **My club** (or **My region**) |
| The federation runs DivingHQ | The federation's admins | **User Manager**, on the **Pending** tab |

The card tells you who it's waiting for, for example *"Waiting for Bondi Diving Club's admins to confirm"*. You don't need to know who those people are or how to reach them: DivingHQ sends them a notification and an email the moment you add your child. There's no fixed wait, it depends on when they next look. If a few days go by, mention it to the coach or at the pool; whoever runs the club's admin side will know.

While you wait:

- your child can't be entered in meets yet
- you can **Withdraw** the request if you made a mistake (nothing is kept)

When it's decided, you get a notification:

![The inbox with one notification, "Mia Fischer's junior profile is confirmed", saying Mia can be entered in meets now](/guide-screenshots/juniors-inbox.png)

If the club turns it down, the profile is removed. Usually that means they didn't recognise the details, so get in touch with them and try again.

## If your child is already on DivingHQ

Your child's club, or their coach, may have added them before you got there. You don't need to find out, or work out who to ask: **add your child the normal way**.

- If the **name, date of birth and club** all match a profile that's already here, DivingHQ doesn't make a second one. It asks the club to link you to the existing profile, and you get a notification once they have. You don't see anything about that profile that you didn't type yourself.
- If the name is spelled differently ("Mia" against "Mia Rose"), your request goes to the club as usual. They see the profile they already have with the same birthday, press **Same child**, and you're linked to it; the duplicate is removed. You get a notification saying your child was already on DivingHQ.

If the club does ask for your DivingHQ **username** (the one you sign in with), it's shown at the top of **Junior divers**. They can add you under **Parents** on your child's card with it.

Is there a second parent? Once you're linked, add them the same way: type their username under **Parents** and press **Add a parent**. They need their own account first, and the club approves them, just as it did you.

## Once your child is confirmed

![A confirmed junior card: Mia Fischer, aged 9, Bondi Diving Club, "You're their parent", with the parents list, an "Add a parent" box, and Edit and Delete profile buttons](/guide-screenshots/juniors-confirmed.png)

### Entering meets

Juniors are entered in meets like every other diver, by the people who do that already: your child's **coach** (a coach linked to your child can submit their dive lists from **Coaching**) or the **meet's organisers** from Meet Manager. Ask the club how they handle entries. Your child doesn't get a Dive Sheets page of their own, because nobody signs in to a junior profile.

### Results

Your child's name, club and scores appear on scoreboards, results and records exactly like everyone else's. What's different is the profile page: for a diver under 13 it isn't public, and they don't come up in the diver search. Only you, their coaches, their club and the federation can open it.

### Notifications

Everything that would go to your child goes to you instead, with their name in front, for example *"Mia Fischer: Your event is live"*. That includes events starting, results going up, moving through to a final, a reserve place turning into a start, and their coach submitting or changing a dive list. You also get the "meet is live" and "results posted" emails.

To get them on your phone, turn notifications on in **Settings** (see [FAQ → "How do I turn notifications on or off?"](/guide/faq#how-do-i-turn-notifications-on-or-off)). Everything also lands in your **Inbox**.

### Changing details or removing a profile

- **Edit** fixes your child's name, date of birth or gender.
- Moving to another club is up to the club or the federation; ask them.
- **Delete profile** removes your child's profile. If they've already dived in a meet, their name stays on those results, because they're part of that meet's record.
- **Take me off** unlinks you from your child (the other parent and the club stay).

## When your child turns 13

From their 13th birthday your child can look after their own profile. A **Hand over** box appears on their card:

![Leon Fischer's card, aged 13, with "Hand over to Leon Fischer": an explanation, an email address typed in and a "Send the link" button](/guide-screenshots/juniors-handover.png)

1. Type **your child's own email address** (it becomes the address they sign in with) and press **Send the link**.
2. They get an email with a link that works once, for 7 days. **Send again** replaces it; **Cancel the link** stops it.
3. They open the link, choose a username and password, tick that they're 13 or over, and press **Set up my account**:

![The "Take over your profile" page for Leon Fischer: the email address they'll sign in with, then username, password, confirm password, the 13-or-older tick and "Set up my account"](/guide-screenshots/junior-handover-page.png)

That's it. Every result, record and club detail stays with them, and they can sign in straight away. From then on their notifications go to them, not you, and they leave your **Junior divers** list. You stay linked as their parent, so you can still pay for them where your federation uses DivingHQ for fees.

There's no deadline. Until you hand over, the profile simply stays a junior profile. Please don't have your child sign up for a fresh account instead: it would start empty, without their results.

## For coaches

- Add a junior from **Junior divers** and choose **Their coach**. Whoever confirms the club's divers checks it, and once confirmed you're linked to them as their coach.
- Linked juniors appear in **Coaching** with your other divers, so you can enter them in meets and submit their dive lists.
- You'll see them on **Junior divers** too, but changing their details is for their parents and the club.

## For club and region admins

Where your country has no federation on DivingHQ yet, you're the one who confirms new juniors, in **Junior divers to confirm** on **My club** (region admins: **My region**):

![The "Junior divers to confirm" list on My club: Mia Fischer, 9, female, added by Anna Fischer, her parent, with Turn down and Confirm buttons](/guide-screenshots/juniors-club-confirm.png)

- **Confirm** only if you know the diver is in your club and the person who added them is who they say they are. Confirming lets the junior be entered in meets and links the parent (or coach) who added them.
- **Turn down** removes the profile. The person who added it is told.
- A warning shows when someone in your club already has the same date of birth, naming them. If it's the same child under another spelling, press **Same child**: the person who added the new one is linked to the existing profile (as its parent, or its coach) and the duplicate is removed. That's how a parent gets linked without having to know who runs the club.
- Parents asking to be linked to an existing junior wait in the same list.
- Nobody confirms something they're part of: if you added your own child as their parent, another admin, the region or DivingHQ confirms it.

You can add juniors yourself too. Choose **The club's admin**: where the clubs run the country, they're confirmed at once. Every junior in your club is on your **Junior divers** page, where you can fix details, add parents by username and hand a profile over at 13.

Under a federation, club admins still add and look after the club's juniors, but the federation's admins confirm them.

## For federation admins

Juniors waiting for you are in **User Manager**, on the **Pending** tab, under **Junior divers to confirm**:

![User Manager's "Junior divers to confirm" list: Noah Becker, 10, male, Berlin Diving Club, added by his coach, with Turn down and Confirm buttons](/guide-screenshots/juniors-federation-confirm.png)

The same rules apply: confirm only juniors you can vouch for through their club. Juniors you add yourself (as **The federation's admin**) are confirmed at once.

## Privacy and safety

- **What's kept:** your child's name, date of birth, gender and club, plus their results. There's no email address, password or photo, and nobody can sign in to the profile.
- **Who sees what:** anyone can see the name, club and scores on results and scoreboards, the same as for every diver. The profile page and the diver search are limited to the child's parents, coaches, club and federation.
- **The agreement:** each time someone adds a junior, who ticked the agreement and when is recorded.
- **Concerns:** if something about a child's profile or results worries you, write to [support@divinghq.app](mailto:support@divinghq.app) with the details (include the meet if it's about a result). See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for the full picture.

## Questions parents ask

### "Can my child sign in on my phone?"

No. Accounts are for people 13 and over, and nobody should sign in with someone else's account. Your child doesn't need to sign in to follow a meet: scoreboards and results are public, no account needed.

### "My child is 13 or older"

They can [create an account](/register) of their own. If they already have a junior profile, [hand it over](#when-your-child-turns-13) instead so their results come with them.

### "Who runs my child's club on DivingHQ? How do I contact them?"

DivingHQ doesn't list the club's admins for parents, and you don't need them: everything you send (adding your child, asking to be linked) goes to them automatically, and your child's card says who it's waiting for. If something seems stuck, ask the coach or someone at the pool; they'll know who does the club's admin. Still stuck? Write to [support@divinghq.app](mailto:support@divinghq.app) with your child's name and club and we'll chase it.

### "I can't find my child in the diver search"

That's on purpose: divers under 13 don't appear in it. Your child's card on **Junior divers** opens their profile.

### "I'm not getting my child's notifications"

Check that your child's card says **Confirmed** and that you're listed under **Parents** without a "waiting for approval" badge. Then check **Settings** → Notifications on the device you're using.
